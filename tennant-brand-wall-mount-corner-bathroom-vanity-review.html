The headline feature on the Tennant Brand vanity is its corner-mounted shape. Instead of taking up a flat stretch of wall the way the eclife, LIKIMIO and OWIPAW vanities do, it tucks into a 90-degree corner and mounts directly to the wall rather than resting on the floor. That matters most in small bathrooms where every standard vanity under consideration is simply too wide to clear the door, the toilet or a tub. At 18 inches, it is also the smallest of the four vanities in this tennant brand wall mount corner bathroom vanity comparison, which is the entire point: this cabinet is built for the bathrooms where the other three options would not fit at all, not for someone comparing storage volume across similarly sized vanities.

Construction uses MDF, or engineered wood, the same base material listed for several vanities in this price range. Because the cabinet mounts to the wall rather than standing on four legs, the mounting hardware and the strength of the wall anchors matter more here than they would for a floor vanity, so confirming stud placement before drilling is worth the extra few minutes. At $295.00, the Tennant Brand vanity costs close to the same as the eclife, LIKIMIO and OWIPAW alternatives, which means the choice between them has little to do with price and almost everything to do with whether your bathroom has a corner to fill or a flat wall that can take a wider cabinet.
