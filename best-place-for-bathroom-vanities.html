Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best place for bathroom vanities online?

What is the difference between the WindBay 30 inch and 36 inch vanities?
Both are wall-mount floating vanities from the same brand, but the 36-inch model adds six inches of width and counter space for $70 more than the 30-inch version. Is a floating vanity or a farmhouse vanity a better choice?
It depends on your bathroom and budget. Reviewers consistently note both styles hold up well when installed correctly, so the choice comes down to look and installation preference rather than one being objectively better.
