Frequently Asked Questions

Is walnut a good finish for a bathroom vanity?
Walnut and walnut-toned finishes hold up well in bathrooms because darker wood tones hide water spots, toothpaste splatter, and everyday scuffs better than white or light gray cabinets. Most of the best walnut bathroom vanities we compared use an MDF or engineered-wood cabinet with a walnut-look finish rather than solid walnut wood, which keeps cost down while still giving you the warmer look.
What size walnut bathroom vanity do I need?
It depends on your bathroom and whether you want one sink or two. The vanities in this comparison range from a 36-inch single-sink DELUXE LIVING cabinet for small bathrooms up to 72-inch double-sink options from XWNE and Alphabath for primary bathrooms, so measure your available wall space, including door swing and fixtures, before choosing a width.
Are floating walnut vanities harder to install than floor-standing ones?
Yes, generally. Floating vanities like the Floating Vanity 36-inch with Smart and the LUTHXAY Floating Bathroom Vanity with Makeup in this comparison mount to the wall and need blocking behind the drywall to support the cabinet's weight, while floor-standing options like the T4TREAM and DELUXE LIVING vanities connect to your existing plumbing rough-in without that extra step.
