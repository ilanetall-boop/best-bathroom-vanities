Things to Know Before You Buy

  • "24-inch vanity cabinet" covers a range of widths. Only one of the seven cabinets we compared, the Yaheetech, is a true 24 inches wide; the rest run from 28 to 60 inches for readers who have a bit more wall space.
  • MDF dominates the budget and mid-range tiers. Five of the seven cabinets use MDF or engineered wood construction, which keeps cost down but means standing water near the base should be wiped up quickly.
  • ARIEL cabinets ship as complete quartz-topped units. Both ARIEL Hepburn models and the ARIEL Taylor include a matched quartz counter, so the higher price covers the full setup, not the cabinet alone.
  • Review sample size varies widely. Ratings range from a 3-review ONBRILL listing to a 95-review Yaheetech listing, so a high star average on a thin sample deserves a second look before you buy.
  • Sink configuration changes with width. Only the 60-inch ARIEL Hepburn cabinet offers a double-sink layout; every other cabinet in this comparison is single-sink.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a true 24-inch vanity cabinet good for?
A 24-inch vanity cabinet works best in powder rooms, guest bathrooms, and any space where a wider cabinet would crowd the door, toilet, or shower. Among the best 24-inch vanity cabinets we compared, the Yaheetech is the only model built to that exact width; every other cabinet here is 28 inches or wider.
Do vanity cabinets in this size range include a countertop?
It depends on the listing. The ARIEL cabinets in this comparison ship as complete units with a quartz counter included, while the Yaheetech, ANGRYWIN, and 36-inch budget pick are sold as the cabinet only, so you'll need to budget separately for a countertop and sink.
Is it worth sizing up from a 24-inch cabinet if my bathroom allows it?
If your bathroom has the wall space, sizing up to a 28-, 31-, or 36-inch cabinet like the ONBRILL, ANGRYWIN, or ARIEL Hepburn 36-inch typically means more storage and a wider counter for a modest price increase. Reviewers consistently note the extra width is worth it once a bathroom can accommodate it, but a strict 24-inch footprint still calls for a true 24-inch cabinet like the Yaheetech.
