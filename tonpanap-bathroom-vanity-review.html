What Could Be Better

The biggest gap in this Tonpanap bathroom vanity review is rating data. Three of the four listings we compared, including the Tonpanap itself, do not publish a star rating or review count, so we cannot point to a verified review history the way we could for an older, more established product. That puts more weight on the specs that are available: MDF construction, standard 36-inch sizing and price.

Engineered wood is also a real tradeoff worth naming directly. MDF handles normal bathroom humidity fine, but sustained water exposure around the sink cutout or a slow leak will damage it faster than solid wood or marine-grade plywood would. Anyone who has dealt with a leaking faucet before should budget for careful caulking and prompt repairs rather than treating this vanity, or any of its MDF-built competitors here, as fully water-resistant.
