Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a true 36-inch vanity cabinet?
A true 36-inch vanity cabinet measures exactly 36 inches across the cabinet body, not the countertop overhang. Among the seven cabinets we compared, only the ARIEL Hepburn 36-inch Bathroom Vanity is built to that exact width; the rest run from 24 to 60 inches for readers whose bathrooms have a little more or less wall space to work with.
Do 36-inch vanity cabinets come with the countertop included?
It depends on the listing. Both ARIEL cabinets in this comparison ship as complete units with a matched quartz counter, while the SUNTAGE, DELUXE LIVING, Yaheetech, and Amada cabinets are sold as the cabinet only, so you'll need to budget separately for a countertop and sink.
Is MDF or solid wood better for a bathroom vanity cabinet?
MDF, or engineered wood, is what every cabinet in this comparison uses, and it keeps cost down while holding paint and veneer finishes evenly. The tradeoff is that standing water near the base or sink cutout needs to be wiped up quickly, since MDF swells if moisture sits against it for long periods, which solid wood resists somewhat better at a higher price.
