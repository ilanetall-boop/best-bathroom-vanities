Can a floating vanity like the Ambrovina hold the same weight as a floor-mounted one?

It depends on the wall anchoring more than the cabinet itself. A floating vanity needs to be mounted into wall studs or with heavy-duty anchors rated for the combined weight of the cabinet, countertop, and stored items, so confirm your bathroom wall can support that before installing. Is MDF construction a dealbreaker for a bathroom vanity?

Not if you keep standing water off the surface. MDF and engineered wood, like the core in the Ambrovina, hold up fine under normal bathroom humidity, but they swell faster than solid wood when water sits on the surface for hours.
