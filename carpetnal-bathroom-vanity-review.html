Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Vanity width should match your existing plumbing rough-in. Moving drain and supply lines adds significant cost, so measure before ordering any 48 inch or 60 inch model.
  • MDF and engineered wood cabinets, like the one on the Carpetnal bathroom vanity in this review, hold up well in a dry climate but need sealed edges and prompt cleanup near the sink to avoid moisture damage.
  • Freestanding farmhouse-style vanities, such as the AMERLIFE, sit on exposed legs and clear the floor easily, while framed cabinet vanities like the Carpetnal and the Oakvera cover more floor space but add storage.
  • Ready-to-assemble vanities like the Quicklock ship flat and cost less to move, but factor in assembly time and basic tools before you commit to a delivery date.
  • Current Amazon prices for 60 inch vanities in this category cluster between $439.99 and $449.99 as of this review, so an eleven dollar gap between models usually comes down to finish, not footprint.
