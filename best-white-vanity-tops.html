Frequently Asked Questions

Do white vanity tops show water spots and stains more than other colors?
A glossy white ceramic or SMC sink top can show mineral spots from hard water more visibly than a darker finish, but the glazed surfaces on the picks in this guide, like the ELEVACHIC and LIKIMIO sinks, are designed to resist stains and buildup. Wiping up standing water near the sink and cabinet seams promptly keeps a white top looking clean over time.
Does a white vanity top come with the sink already built in?
Yes, all seven vanities in this comparison ship with an integrated sink. Six use a glazed ceramic basin, and the Merax 48-inch pick uses an SMC sink top, so you won't need to buy or install a separate basin.
What size white vanity top fits a small bathroom?
The ALBAD 16-Inch Modern Bathroom Vanity in this guide is the narrowest option and fits a small bathroom, apartment or dorm room where a standard 30-inch top would crowd the door. If you have a bit more space, the 30-inch LIKIMIO and ELEVACHIC picks fit most standard bathroom openings.
