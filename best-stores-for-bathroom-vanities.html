Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best store for buying a bathroom vanity?
Among general retailers, Amazon is a strong pick because it combines a wide selection of vanities and storage cabinets with documented pricing and dimensions in one place. In this comparison, the Homhedy Farmhouse Bathroom Storage Cabinet is our top pick from that selection at $79.99.
Should I buy a vanity cabinet alone or a vanity-and-sink combo?
It depends on whether your existing sink and countertop still work. A combo like the Mirightone 30-inch vanity or the eclife 36-inch vanity includes the sink and countertop, and in eclife's case a matching faucet, which simplifies shopping. A storage cabinet like the Homhedy or TEENFON pick works if your current sink and counter are fine and you only need more storage.
Is it cheaper to build a bathroom vanity than to buy one?
It can be, but it depends on your tools, skill, and the materials you choose. The $14.45 DIY Cabinet Building for Beginners guide is the cheapest entry point in this comparison, but building a vanity yourself still costs money in lumber, hardware, and a countertop, and it takes far more time than ordering a finished cabinet like the seven other picks here.
