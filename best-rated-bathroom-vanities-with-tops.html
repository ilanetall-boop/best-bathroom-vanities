Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Measure twice, including the door swing. A 30" vanity clears most powder rooms, but anything over 48" needs a clear width check against your door and any adjacent fixtures.
  • Single sink versus double sink changes the math. The widest vanity here, the 72 x 22 TUKTUK, ships with an integrated double sink, so plan for two sets of plumbing rough-ins unless you're replacing an existing double-sink unit.
  • MDF cabinets need dry conditions. Every vanity in this guide uses MDF or engineered wood construction. It holds up fine under normal use, but standing water left at the base seam will swell it over time.
  • An attached top saves a step, not a trip. Skipping a separate countertop purchase is convenient, but confirm the faucet holes match your existing faucet before you order.
  • Price scales with width more than features. Across this lineup, 30" vanities run $216 to $220, while 59" to 72" models with more counter space run $420 to $690.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a bathroom vanity with tops different from a cabinet-only vanity?

A vanity with tops ships with the countertop, and often the sink basin, already attached to the cabinet, so you skip buying and cutting a separate slab. A cabinet-only vanity requires you to source, cut and install the countertop and sink separately, which adds cost and installation time.

What size bathroom vanity with a top should I buy?

Measure your available wall width first, then subtract a few inches for clearance around doors and adjacent fixtures. In this comparison, 30-inch models fit powder rooms and small bathrooms, 36 to 47.2 inches works for standard single-sink bathrooms, and 59 to 72 inches suits primary bathrooms that need a double sink.

Are MDF bathroom vanities durable in a humid bathroom?

Yes, with normal use. Every vanity in this guide uses MDF or engineered wood cabinet construction, which holds up fine as long as standing water gets wiped up rather than left to sit at the base seams. In owner reviews, assembly quality comes up more often than the base material as the thing that affects long-term durability at this price point.
