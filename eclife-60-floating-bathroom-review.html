Frequently Asked Questions

Does the eclife 60" floating vanity include a faucet and drain?

Yes. The eclife 60" floating bathroom vanity ships as a sink combo, so the double white sink, faucet, and drain are included in the $549.99 price. You will still need to add a mirror and connect the drain lines during installation.

Can a floating vanity like the eclife hold the same weight as a floor-mounted cabinet?

It depends on your wall, not the cabinet. A wall-mounted vanity needs to anchor into the studs or use heavy-duty hardware rated for the combined weight of the cabinet, countertop, and whatever you store inside, so check your bathroom wall before mounting this eclife 60" floating bathroom vanity or any other floating model.

Is MDF construction a problem for a bathroom vanity?

Not under normal use. The eclife's MDF and engineered wood core handles everyday bathroom humidity fine, but it swells faster than the solid wood used in the CozyHommie alternatives below if water sits on the surface for hours, so wipe up spills around the double sink promptly.
