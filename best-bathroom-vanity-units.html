Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Size varies more than the category name suggests. The seven vanity units we compared run from an 18-inch drawer unit to a 42-inch cabinet, so measure your wall space before assuming any "36-inch" listing fits your bathroom exactly.
  • MDF and engineered wood are the standard build materials. Every cabinet in this comparison, including the CozyHommie model marketed with a solid-wood-style front, is built from MDF or engineered wood per its listed specs.
  • RTA (ready-to-assemble) cabinets need tools and time. The Design House Brookings and CozyHommie units ship flat-packed, so budget an afternoon and a screwdriver before you can install either one.
  • Two OWIPAW cabinets cover different budgets. The brand's 36-inch cabinet costs about $48 more than its similarly sized single-sink alternative, so compare both before choosing.
  • Price spans a wide range. At $169.99 to $539.99, the gap between the cheapest and priciest vanity units here is over $370, driven mostly by size and included features rather than brand name.

The best bathroom vanity units balance material, size, and price differently depending on whether your bathroom needs a compact drawer unit or a wider cabinet with room for two people to get ready at once. We compared seven vanity units ranging from an 18-inch shaker-style drawer to a 42-inch solid-wood-style cabinet, weighing construction, dimensions, and price against what buyers actually report after installing each one. Our top pick, the Design House Brookings 36 Inch, earned that spot by pairing a full 36-inch RTA cabinet with a price under $420, but the other six cover smaller spaces, tighter budgets, and different finish styles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I look for in the best bathroom vanity units?
Start with width, since the seven units we compared range from 18 to 42 inches, and a cabinet that's too wide for your bathroom will crowd the door or toilet. After that, check whether the price includes a countertop, since most cabinets here, including our top pick, sell the cabinet alone, and confirm whether it ships RTA (ready-to-assemble) or pre-built, since that changes how much setup time to budget.
Are RTA (ready-to-assemble) bathroom vanities as sturdy as pre-assembled ones?
Based on verified owner reviews, RTA cabinets like the Design House Brookings and CozyHommie hold up as well as pre-built options once assembled correctly, since the underlying material, MDF or engineered wood, is the same either way. The main difference is the assembly step itself: RTA cabinets need a screwdriver and some time before they're ready to install, while pre-built cabinets arrive ready to mount.
How much should a bathroom vanity unit cost?
In this comparison, prices range from $169.99 for the ModernMate 30-inch cabinet to $539.99 for the CozyHommie 42-inch cabinet. Most of that gap comes from size rather than brand, since every unit here uses similar MDF and engineered wood construction. A reasonable mid-range budget for a 36-inch cabinet, based on our top pick and the two OWIPAW listings, runs from about $285 to $415.
