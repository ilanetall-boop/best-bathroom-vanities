Frequently Asked Questions

What countertop pairs best with a gray bathroom vanity?
White or light-veined quartz creates the strongest contrast against a gray cabinet, while a matching gray-toned quartz keeps the look monochrome. Among the vanities we compared, the 48-inch pick and the Gray Shaker Bathroom Vanity Sink already ship with a counter included, so you don't need to source one separately for either of those two. For a cabinet-only pick like the eclife, check our sizing guide before ordering a top.
Do I need a full vanity, or will a cabinet work with my existing sink?
If your sink and counter are already in good shape, a cabinet like the HOMCOM or TEENFON pick adds storage without replacing what you have. If your sink, counter, and cabinet all need replacing together, the Gray Shaker Bathroom Vanity Sink, the 48-inch vanity, or the Design House Brookings give you a complete unit in one purchase.
Is a 60-inch vanity too big for anything but a primary bathroom?
Yes, in most cases. At 60 x 21 inches, the Design House Brookings pick in this comparison needs primary-bathroom floor space and works best with a double-sink layout. For a secondary or guest bathroom, the 36-inch eclife or Gray Shaker vanities in this comparison, or our 36-inch vanity guide, are a closer fit.
