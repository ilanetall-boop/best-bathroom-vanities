The DELUXE LIVING 36 Inch Modern vanity is built around storage first. The brown melamine finish over MDF is rated to resist the steam and splashes of daily showers, which matters more in a 36-inch vanity than in a larger piece simply because it sits closer to the shower spray in most layouts.

What We Liked

The storage layout is the strongest argument for this vanity in our deluxe living modern bathroom vanity review. Two soft-close doors plus three full-extension drawers beat the single-cabinet layout you find on a lot of competing 36-inch vanities, and soft-close hardware means you avoid the slam and wear that cheaper hinges develop over a few years of daily use. The seamless composite sink and countertop is the other standout feature. Because the basin and counter are molded as one piece, there is no seam line to collect grime, which is a common complaint on vanities with a separately mounted sink.

We also like that the countertop ships pre-drilled with both 4 inch and 8 inch faucet spacing options and includes a drill bit for adjustments. That flexibility matters if you are reusing an existing faucet rather than buying a new one to match the vanity, and it is a detail the eclife and LIKIMIO listings do not call out as clearly.
