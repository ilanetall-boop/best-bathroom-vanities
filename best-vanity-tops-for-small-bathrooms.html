Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best vanity top size for a small bathroom?
Most small bathrooms work best with a 24-inch-wide vanity top, like the VINGLI pick in this guide, since anything from 28 inches up tends to crowd a nearby door, toilet, or shower in a tight layout. If your bathroom measures wider than that, a 30- or 36-inch vanity gives you more counter space without the same compromise.
Do small-bathroom vanity tops need extra accessories to make the space work?
Often yes. A small counter fills up fast once soap, a toothbrush cup, and everyday items sit on it, which is why a compact organizer like the Bamboo Toothbrush Holder in this comparison can matter as much as the vanity top itself. A dedicated piece like the RIHHA vanity desk can also take pressure off the main sink counter if your bathroom has room for a second surface.
Should I replace the tub or toilet at the same time as the vanity top?
Only if your remodel budget and floor plan call for it. The 59 Inch Acrylic Freestanding Bathtub and the two toilets in this comparison are included for readers doing a fuller small-bathroom renovation, not as required pairings with a new vanity top. Most small bathrooms see a meaningful upgrade from the vanity top alone.
