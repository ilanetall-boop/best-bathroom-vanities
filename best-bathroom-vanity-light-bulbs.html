Frequently Asked Questions

Will new light bulbs fix dim bathroom vanity lighting on their own?
Sometimes, but not always. If your current bulbs are old or the wrong color temperature, replacing them helps. But if the shadows come from a single centered fixture or a mirror mounted too close to the wall, a bulb swap will not fix the angle problem. Products like the FENNIO Vanity Mirror with Lights or the Led Vanity Mirror Lights Hollywood kit address the fixture position directly instead of the bulb alone.
What is the best light bulb color temperature for a bathroom vanity?
Most reviewers and lighting guides point to a range between 3000K and 5000K for vanity use, since that range renders skin tones accurately without looking clinical. If you are shopping for a standalone bulb rather than one of the fixtures in this guide, look for the color temperature listed in Kelvin on the packaging rather than vague terms like warm or bright.
Should I buy a new vanity or upgrade the lighting instead?
It depends on your counter space. If your current vanity already has room on both sides of the mirror, an LED strip kit like the Led Vanity Mirror Lights Hollywood option is the cheaper fix. If clutter or a narrow counter is forcing your only light source into one spot, a wider vanity like the 30" Bathroom Vanity with Sink or the Ambrovina 48" Floating Bathroom Vanity solves the layout problem the bulb cannot.
