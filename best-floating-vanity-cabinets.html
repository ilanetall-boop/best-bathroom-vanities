Frequently Asked Questions

What separates a floating vanity cabinet from one only styled to look wall-mounted?
A true floating vanity cabinet mounts directly to wall studs or a reinforced backing board and has no legs or base touching the floor. Among the seven cabinets we compared, only the 30" Bathroom Vanity with Sink, the SUNTAGE, and the AMERLIFE confirm that wall-mounted hardware in their listings; the HOPUBUY and JUMMICO are freestanding cabinets that share the visual style without the actual wall mount.
Can you install a floating vanity cabinet without hitting wall studs?
No. Floating cabinets need to anchor into wall studs or a reinforced backing, since drywall alone can't support the weight of a mounted cabinet plus a sink full of water. If you can't locate studs in the right spot, a freestanding option like the HOPUBUY or JUMMICO in this comparison avoids the issue entirely. Our installation guide walks through the stud-finding process.
Why does one of the picks in this guide not include storage?
The WYRNOBRX is a vessel sink, not a full vanity cabinet, and we included it because it's built specifically for a floating countertop setup, which is a common path for buyers researching this category. If you need storage as well as a sink, any of the other six picks above is the better fit.
