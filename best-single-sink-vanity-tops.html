Frequently Asked Questions

What size single-sink vanity top fits a small bathroom?
The ROOMTEC 24-inch pick in this guide is the narrowest option and fits a small bathroom, powder room or half bath where a standard 36-inch vanity would crowd the door. If you have more space, the 36-inch OWIPAW, eclife, T4TREAM and chartustriable picks fit most standard bathroom openings.
Do single-sink vanity tops come with the sink already installed?
Yes, all seven vanities in this comparison ship with an integrated single sink, so you won't need to buy or install a separate basin. Faucets are typically sold separately, so check the listing before you budget for the full project.
Should I choose a single-sink or double-sink vanity top?
A single-sink vanity top like the picks in this guide works well for a guest bathroom, powder room or a primary bathroom used by one person at a time. If two people regularly get ready at the same time, a double-sink layout may be worth the extra width and cost.
