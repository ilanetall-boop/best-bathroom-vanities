Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard width for a single-sink bathroom vanity?
Single-sink bathroom vanities in this comparison range from 30 to 48 inches wide, with 30 and 36 inches the most common widths for a full bathroom. A 30-inch vanity like the Mirightone or Amada fits a standard 5-by-8-foot bathroom and lines up with our 30-inch bathroom vanities guide, while a 36-inch or 48-inch model, like the eclife or DELUXE LIVING, works better in a larger primary bathroom with room to spare.
Are single-sink vanities cheaper than double-sink vanities?
Yes, generally. A single-sink vanity uses one basin and typically a narrower cabinet, which keeps material and shipping costs lower than a double-sink vanity of comparable width. In this comparison, prices range from $169.95 for the ELEVACHIC 30-inch pick to $1,059.99 for the 48-inch DELUXE LIVING model, with most single-sink options landing between $200 and $320.
What material are most affordable single-sink vanities made from?
All seven vanities in this comparison use MDF or engineered wood for the cabinet and top, which is standard at this price point. MDF keeps costs down but is more sensitive to standing water than solid wood, so wipe up spills around the sink basin promptly to avoid swelling at the seams.
