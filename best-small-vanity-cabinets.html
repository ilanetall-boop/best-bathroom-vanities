Frequently Asked Questions

What's a good width for a small vanity cabinet?
Most of the best small vanity cabinets fall in the 24- to 30-inch range, which fits powder rooms, guest bathrooms, and any space where a 36-inch cabinet would crowd the door or toilet. The Bathroom Vanity with Sink 24 and both FUvellamo cabinets in this comparison hold to a true 24-inch width, while the VINGLI comes in at 30 inches for slightly more counter space. See our 24-inch vanity guide for more options at that specific width.
Do small vanity cabinets come with a sink included?
It depends on the listing. The Bathroom Vanity with Sink 24 and both FUvellamo options ship with an integrated sink top, while the VINGLI 30 Inch Bathroom Vanity and the DELUXE LIVING 60 Inch Bathroom vanity list their own sink configurations separately on Amazon, so check the product page before you budget for a countertop.
Is MDF a durable material for a small bathroom vanity?
MDF, or medium-density fiberboard, is the construction material behind most of the small vanity cabinets in this comparison, and it's standard for this price and size category. It holds up well under normal use, but owners report that standing water left near the base or sink cutout can cause swelling over time, so wiping up spills promptly extends the cabinet's life.
