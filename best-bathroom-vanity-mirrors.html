Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Measure before you shop. Vanity width determines how much mirror space you'll have above it. A 48-inch vanity needs a proportionally wide mirror or a pair of smaller mirrors.
  • Pedestal storage isn't a vanity replacement. The Bonnlo cabinets add shelving around an existing pedestal sink but won't give you a full countertop.
  • MDF and engineered wood dominate this price range. Solid wood vanities cost significantly more; look for a sealed, moisture-resistant finish instead.
  • Barn-door and drawer-front styles affect clearance. A sliding barn door needs open wall space beside the vanity, while drawers need room to fully extend.
  • Some vanities ship without a countertop or sink. Check whether the price includes a top, basin, and faucet, or whether you'll need to source those separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size vanity should I buy for a small bathroom?
For bathrooms under 40 square feet, look at pedestal or compact storage cabinets in the 24-30 inch range, like the Bonnlo pedestal storage cabinets or the Yaheetech 30-inch vanity, rather than a full 48-inch double-sink unit.
Do these vanities include a mirror?
No. Every pick here is a vanity or storage cabinet only; you'll pair it with a separate wall mirror or medicine cabinet sized to match the vanity's width.
What material holds up best in a humid bathroom?
Moisture-resistant MDF and engineered wood with a sealed finish, like most of the vanities on this list, perform well as long as spills are wiped up promptly and the cabinet isn't installed in direct contact with standing water.
