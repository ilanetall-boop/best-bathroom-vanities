  • Measure your actual wall space first. Cabinet widths in this comparison span from 18 to 24.5 inches, and a difference of a few inches determines whether a vanity clears your toilet or door swing. Check our sizing guide before you buy.
  • Floating vanities buy back floor space. Three of the seven picks here, the AmbroVania, Malwee, and Beingnext, mount to the wall instead of standing on the floor, which makes a small bathroom easier to clean and feel larger.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best bathroom vanity for small spaces?
Among the seven vanities we compared, the Yaheetech 24.5 Inch Modern Bathroom is the best bathroom vanity for small spaces for most buyers, pairing a full-size 24.5-inch footprint with a $149.99 price and the largest verified review sample in this comparison. If your bathroom is narrower than that, the 18-inch Malwee floating vanity is a better fit.
Should I choose a floating or freestanding vanity for a small bathroom?
A floating vanity like the AmbroVania, Malwee, or Beingnext frees up floor space and makes a small bathroom easier to clean, but it typically requires wall anchoring during installation. A freestanding cabinet like the Yaheetech or SUNTAGE sets up more simply and usually costs less for a comparable width.
How narrow can a bathroom vanity get?
The narrowest vanity in this comparison is the 18-inch Malwee, though it trades some counter and storage space for that footprint. The Beingnext splits the difference at 22 inches, while most of the remaining picks here run close to 24 inches, the width where a vanity starts to offer a full-size sink basin.
