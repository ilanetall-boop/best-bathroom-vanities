Things to Know Before You Buy

  • MDF dominates this price range. All four vanities and cabinets in this comparison use MDF or engineered wood construction, which keeps cost down at the expense of moisture tolerance if water sits at the base.
  • Not every listing includes a sink. The eclife vanities ship with a sink top, while the TEENFON and Bonnlo storage cabinets do not, so check each listing before you budget for a separate countertop.
  • Pedestal storage cabinets aren't full vanities. The TEENFON and Bonnlo cabinets are built to wrap around an existing pedestal sink rather than replace it, a different job than a standalone vanity.
  • A $1000 budget covers more than the cabinet. Two picks below, a foot spa and a comfort-height toilet, aren't vanities, but they help you plan a fuller bathroom remodel around the same budget.
  • Measure before you order. Vanity width, plus the footprint of any pedestal cabinet, should be checked against your floor space and existing plumbing before purchase.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's a realistic budget for a good bathroom vanity under $1000?
Based on this comparison, a solid single-sink vanity with an included sink top runs $280 to $500, like the eclife models here. If you already have a pedestal sink and only need storage, a cabinet like the TEENFON or Bonnlo options costs under $100, which leaves most of a $1000 budget for a toilet, faucet, or lighting upgrade elsewhere in the bathroom.
Are pedestal sink storage cabinets the same as bathroom vanities?
No. A pedestal sink storage cabinet, like the TEENFON and Bonnlo picks in this guide, wraps storage around a sink you already own rather than replacing the sink and cabinet as a unit. A full vanity, like the eclife models here, includes the cabinet and typically the sink top in one purchase. Choose a storage cabinet if your existing pedestal sink is in good shape and you need somewhere to put toiletries.
Is MDF good enough for a bathroom vanity?
MDF, or medium-density fiberboard, is the material used in every vanity and cabinet in this comparison, and it's standard for this price range. It holds up well under normal use, but owners report that standing water left near the base or sink cutout can cause swelling over time, so wiping up spills promptly extends the life of any of the best bathroom vanities under $1000 in this guide.
